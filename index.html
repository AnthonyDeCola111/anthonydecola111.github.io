What Is drifting-tts?

drifting-tts is a text-to-speech program built specifically for the Turkish language. You give it written Turkish, and it gives you back a spoken audio file. That is the whole idea — one step in, one audio file out.

The voice inside drifting-tts was trained using a modern method called a "drifting objective." In plain terms, it is a training technique that teaches the voice model to produce a smooth, steady result in a single pass instead of many slow, repeated steps. For you, that means two practical things: audio is generated quickly, and the result sounds like a person reading rather than a machine spelling words out.

You do not need to install Python, configure a server, or understand anything about machine learning to use it. If you can copy and paste text, you can use drifting-tts.

🛠️ If Something Does Not Work

These are the small hiccups people hit most often, and what usually resolves them.

  • The file will not open at all. Check that the download finished completely. If your browser shows the download as unfinished or interrupted, remove the file and download it again.
  • Windows says the app cannot run. Restart your computer, then try once more. A restart clears temporary problems more often than people expect.
  • The program opens but nothing happens when you click the audio button. Make sure the text box is not empty, and confirm the text is in Turkish characters rather than a mix of languages.
  • Your laptop feels sluggish. Close other heavy programs before generating long audio. drifting-tts needs a fair amount of memory, and sharing it with a dozen browser tabs slows everything down.
  • The sound is choppy. Play the saved audio file with your usual media player rather than previewing it inside the application. Players are better at smooth playback.
